fundamentals.revenue:
It belongs to the fundamentals table.
Its rows are those where metric is revenue.
Its semantic type is flow.
Its unit is USD.
Its values can be added across companies or brands.
Its values can be added across periods.
A yearly figure is the sum of its periods.
The aggregation methods allowed for it are sum_over_time, sum_across_entities and ratio_of_sums.
Its period type varies by row; read the period_basis column.
Derivation: A reported single quarter is used as is; otherwise Q2 = YTD6 - Q1, Q3 = YTD9 - YTD6, Q4 = FY - YTD9. Each period must start the day after the previous one ends.
Its periods refer to the fiscal calendar.
Each row is at the company level.
Its source is SEC EDGAR XBRL companyfacts API.
Its source URL template is https://data.sec.gov/api/xbrl/companyfacts/CIK{cik}.json
The accession and filed columns identify the source record.
The concept column holds the source concept name.
It was retrieved on 2026-10-24.
Deduplication rule: Duration facts keep the latest-filed row; balance-sheet facts keep the earliest-filed row, because later filings can mis-date comparatives.
Total revenue as filed with the SEC. Stored as filed in the period_basis column: single quarters (Q), year-to-date rows (YTD6, YTD9) and full-year rows (FY). CMG, WING and YUM report on calendar-year or 52/53-week years ending in late December; DRI's fiscal year ends in late May.
